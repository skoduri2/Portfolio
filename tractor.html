Autonomous Agricultural Tractor

This project focuses on an autonomous tractor platform designed to navigate and operate in a pecan farm. I’ve been working on the software and perception side of the system, where the goal is to improve how the vehicle understands the environment while making the overall autonomy stack easier to develop and test.

A large part of the work is modernization and test infrastructure. I’m helping migrate the platform from ROS 1 to ROS 2 so newer autonomy and perception components can be integrated more cleanly, while also exploring upgrades beyond the existing YOLO-based perception stack. In parallel, I’m creating an Isaac Sim version of the tractor environment so behaviors and sensor configurations can be evaluated in simulation before being tested on the physical vehicle.
